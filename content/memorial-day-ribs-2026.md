It's that time of year kids: time for bbq ribs on Memorial Day! This year it's subduded and K.I.S.S.: 2 racks of St. Louis style ribs, Barbie's famous potato salad, Green Giant Corn-on-the-Cob Mini-Ears of corn, and watermellon for dessert.
## Meat

- 2 racks St. Louis style baby back ribs

## Rub & Sauce

- This year I tried Famous Dave's Rib Rub
- Sweet Baby Ray's bbq sauce

## Sides

- Potato salad
- Green Giant Mini-Ears of corn

## Dessert

- Watermellon

## Directions

- For the ribs, I removed the membranes from both racks, painted them with mustard, sprinked them with Dave's dust, and put them in a 250 F oven for 2 hours - uncovered.
- Then, I painted them with Sweet Baby Ray's bbq sauce, and baked them for an additional 2 hours - covered in heavy-duty aluminum foil.

## Results

- They came out tender and tasted really good.
