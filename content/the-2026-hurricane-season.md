Philip's [annual tweet](https://x.com/philklotzbach/status/2042241120047124732) says CSU calls for a below-normal season with 13 named storms, 6 hurricanes, and 2 major hurricanes. He adds that a “high likelihood of robust [#ElNino](https://x.com/hashtag/ElNino?src=hashtag_click) and associated increases in vertical wind shear \[are\] the primary factor\[s\].”

## References

- From the [National Hurricane Center](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/), the 2026 Atlantic [tropical cyclone names](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/aboutnames.shtml) are: [Arthur](#Arthur), [Bertha](#Bertha), [Cristobal](#Cristobal), [Dolly](#Dolly), [Edouard](#Edouard), [Fay](#Fay), [Gonzalo](#Gonzalo), [Hanna](#Hanna), [Isaias](#Isaias), [Josephine](#Josephine), Kyle, Leah, Marco, Nana, Omar, Paulette, Rene, Sally, Teddy, Vicky, Wilfred.
- The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30.
- The [Saffir-Simpson scale](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/aboutsshws.php) runs from 75 miles per hour to 200 miles per hour.
