## Arthur

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

**6/17/2026 at 1500 UTC**

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<a href="https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/ARTHUR.shtml?" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Tropical Storm Arthur</a> formed about 40 miles east-northeast of Port O'Commor, Texas, with winds of 40 mph moving northeast at 9 mph.

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

**Update 6/17/2026 at 1630 UTC**

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Arthur is about 60 miles east-northeast of Port O'Connor, Texas, with winds of 45 mph moving northeast at 9 mph.

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Meanwhile, there is a Heat Advisory from the top of Oklahoma to the Gulf of Mexico from 6/18 11 AM to 6/19 10 PM. Heat index values of 107 to 114 degrees. We had inches of rain yesterday, and tomorrow it's going to feel like 110 F!

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Post-Tropical Cyclone Arthur is about 35 miles north-northeast of Galveston, Texas, with winds of 35 mph, moving northeast at 9 mph.
