July 4th, 2026, will mark the 250th anniversary of the United States Declaration of Independence - the [Semiquincentennial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Semiquincentennial). Happy 250th Birday America!

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

I remember the 200th anniversary - the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Bicentennial" data-type="link" data-id="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Bicentennial">Bicentannial</a> - in 1976. We lived near Washington D. C., and we went to the Washington Monument to watch the fireworks.

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

There's two organizations planning this year's events for Americans to commemorate and celebrate. [America 250](https://america250.org/) is a congressional commission established in 2016. [Freedom 250](https://www.freedom250.org/) is a public-private organization created by Executive Order from President Trump.

## Great American State Fair

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Of course President Trump is throwing a shindig. He ordered the reflecting pool get a new coat of blue paint; it's already full of green algae. He had a cage built on the White House South Lawn to host "[UFC Freedom 250](https://www.ufc.com/event/ufc-freedom-250), an MMA event held as part of the country's 250th birthday and President Trump's 80th birthday." There was supposed to be concerts, but a lot of the singers dropped out, so I think he's going solo and having a rally.

## America 250 vs. Freedom 250

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

America250 and Freedom 250 are two separate organizations spearheading celebrations for the U.S. 250th anniversary, but they differ significantly in their origins, leadership, and event focus. \[[1](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/14/america250-versus-freedom250-what-to-know-about-the-us-semiquincentennial), [2](https://www.wbaltv.com/article/america-250-vs-freedom-250-whats-the-difference/71464058)\]

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

The Core Differences

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

America250

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<!-- wp:list-item -->

</p>

<li>

**Origins:** A bipartisan non-profit and congressional commission established by law in 2016 to plan the Semiquincentennial.

</li>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->

<li>

**Focus:** Historical and educational events nationwide, including localized state celebrations and "America's Block Party".

</li>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->

<li>

**Funding:** Backed by federal dollars allocated by Congress and private donations. \[[2](https://www.wbaltv.com/article/america-250-vs-freedom-250-whats-the-difference/71464058), [3](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-freedom-250-concert-america-250-difference-artists-cancel-rcna347573)\]

</li>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

</ul>

<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Freedom 250

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<!-- wp:list-item -->

</p>

<li>

**Origins:** Established by President Donald Trump via executive order as a public-private partnership Task Force. Trump serves as chair; Vice President JD Vance is vice chair.

</li>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->

<li>

**Focus:** Large-scale, high-profile spectacles in Washington, D.C., such as the Great American State Fair on the National Mall, UFC events, and World Cup Fan Zones.

</li>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->

<li>

**Funding:** Operates primarily as an outside fundraising arm heavily relying on private and international donations. \[[2](https://www.wbaltv.com/article/america-250-vs-freedom-250-whats-the-difference/71464058)\]

</li>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

</ul>

<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

Controversy and Public Reception

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

The existence of two separate entities has sparked political friction. Because the Trump administration formed a new group while an official bipartisan commission already existed, critics argue Freedom 250 is explicitly political and MAGA-aligned. For example, the Great American State Fair faced backlash and artist dropouts when musicians claimed they were misled about the event's political ties. Watchdog groups have also raised concerns over whether Freedom 250 events are selling political access in exchange for donations. \[[1](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/14/america250-versus-freedom250-what-to-know-about-the-us-semiquincentennial), [2](https://www.wbaltv.com/article/america-250-vs-freedom-250-whats-the-difference/71464058), [6](https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/5900978-freedom-250-bash-celebrates-america/), [7](https://www.reddit.com/r/OutOfTheLoop/comments/1ts31s1/what_is_going_on_with_freedom_250_and_a_maga/)\]
