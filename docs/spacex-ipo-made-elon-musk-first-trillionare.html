SpaceX IPO Made Elon Musk First Trillionare

By chris on 2026-06-12

This morning, Friday, June 12, 2026, Elon Musk became the world's first trillionaire when SpaceX shares (NASDAQ: SPCX) soared on their stock market debut. The initial price was \$150 but quickly climbed to \$160 per share. With so much demand and so little supply - only 4% was going up for auction - some stock indexes changed their rules to accommodate SpaceX.
