The 2025 General Election in Texas

Permalink: 2025-10-12 11:33:00-05:00 by chris in Politics tags: election

Elections will take place Tuesday, November 4, 2025. Early voting is October 20-31. This year there are 17 Texas Constitutional amendments voters will vote on.

Voting deadlines

  • Voter registration deadline: October 6, 2025.
  • Early voting period: October 20, 2025, through October 31, 2025.
  • Absentee ballot request deadline: October 24, 2025.
  • Election Day: Tuesday, November 4, 2025.
