The 2025 Hurricane Season

Permalink: 2025-04-10 17:00:00-05:00 by chris in Environment tags: hurricanes storms weather

We’re starting this post early this year with the annual tweet from Philip that calls for a above-normal year with 17 named storms, 9 hurricanes, and 4 major hurricanes. He adds that a “relatively warm Atlantic and likely absence of #ElNino are the primary reasons.”

NHC

References

  • From the National Hurricane Center, the 2025 Atlantic tropical cyclone names are: [Andrea](#Andrea), [Barry](#Barry), [Chantal](#Chantal), [Dexter](#Dexter), [Erin](#Erin), [Fernand](#Fernand), [Gabrielle](#Gabrielle), [Humberto](#Humberto), [Imelda](#Imelda), [Jerry](#Jerry), [Karen](#Karen), [Lorenzo](#Lorenzo), [Melissa](#Melissa), Nestor, Olga, Pablo, Rebekah, Sebastien, Tanya, Van, Wendy.
  • The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30.
  • The Saffir-Simpson scale runs from 75 miles per hour to 200 miles per hour.

Andrea

6/24/2025 at 1500 UTC

Tropical Storm Andrea formed about 1205 miles west of the Azores with winds of 40 mph and moving east-northeast at 17 mph.

Update 6/24/2025 at 2100 UTC

Andrea is about 1110 miles west of the Azores with winds of 40 mph moving northeast at 20 mph.

Update 6/25/2025 at 0300 UTC - LAST

Andrea becomes a remnant low and is about 985 miles west of the Azores with winds of 35 mph moving east-northeast at 20 mph.

Update 6/30/2025 at 0900 UTC - LAST

Barry dissipates about 100 miles northwest of Tampico, Mexico with winds of 30 mph moving northwest at 12 mph.
