The 2026 Hurricane Season

Permalink: 2026-04-20 17:00:00-05:00 by chris in Environment tags: hurricanes

Philip's annual tweet says CSU calls for a below-normal season with 13 named storms, 6 hurricanes, and 2 major hurricanes. He adds that a “high likelihood of robust #ElNino and associated increases in vertical wind shear [are] the primary factor[s].”

References

  • From the National Hurricane Center, the 2026 Atlantic tropical cyclone names are: [Arthur](#Arthur), [Bertha](#Bertha), [Cristobal](#Cristobal), [Dolly](#Dolly), [Edouard](#Edouard), [Fay](#Fay), [Gonzalo](#Gonzalo), [Hanna](#Hanna), [Isaias](#Isaias), [Josephine](#Josephine), Kyle, Leah, Marco, Nana, Omar, Paulette, Rene, Sally, Teddy, Vicky, Wilfred.
  • The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30.
  • The Saffir-Simpson scale runs from 75 miles per hour to 200 miles per hour.
