The 2026 Hurricane Season

By chris on 2026-04-20

Philip's annual tweet says CSU calls for a below-normal season with 13 named storms, 6 hurricanes, and 2 major hurricanes. He adds that a “high likelihood of robust #ElNino and associated increases in vertical wind shear [are] the primary factor[s].”
